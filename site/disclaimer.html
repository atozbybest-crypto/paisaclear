Disclaimer

PaisaClear explains financial products. It does not recommend them, and it is not an adviser. Read this before you act on anything here.

Last updated 6 November 2026

Not investment advice

The content on this site is general information, not advice tailored to you. We do not know your income, your obligations, your tax position or your goals, so nothing here can tell you what to buy, hold or sell. PaisaClear is not a SEBI-registered investment adviser and does not provide portfolio management services.

Markets and returns

Returns shown for funds and ETFs are calculated from published NAV history and are past performance. Past performance does not predict future returns. The value of a market-linked investment falls as well as rises, and you can get back less than you put in.

Rates change

Every rate on this site carries the date it applied. Small-savings rates are notified quarterly, bond yields move daily, and insurers revise premiums without notice to us. Treat any figure older than the page's stated date as historical.

Calculators are estimates

The calculators use the inputs you provide and the assumptions printed beside each result. They do not account for every rule — tax slabs, surcharge, cess, penalties, revision of rates or changes in regulation. Use them to understand how a product behaves, not to plan an exact outcome.

Health insurance

Plan features on the health insurance pages are the insurer's own description of their product, reproduced without independent verification. We do not compare premiums, and a listing is not an endorsement. Waiting periods, sub-limits, room-rent caps and exclusions in the policy wording decide every claim.

Scam checker

The scam checker matches wording patterns commonly used in financial fraud. It cannot confirm that a message is genuine, and a low score is not a clean bill of health. It has no access to call databases, bank records or any fraud registry, and it does not store what you paste.

Dividend records

Payment dates and amounts are taken from public market data. A long record of paying dividends is history, not a promise: a company can reduce or stop a dividend at any time, and share prices fall as well as rise.

News and third-party links

Headlines on the news page are published by other organisations and linked back to them. We do not write or edit those stories, and a link is not an endorsement of the report.

Affiliate relationships

PaisaClear does not currently earn commission from any product on this site. If that changes, affected pages will say so in plain language at the top, and payment will never influence a rating or a ranking.

If something here looks wrong, tell us through the contact form — corrections take priority.
