    What this game teaches, and what it does not

    • Fake everything. The companies do not exist, the prices are generated by a random walk, and no money is involved. This is a teaching toy, not a market feed.
    • What it shows well: why buying after a rally is a bad habit, why selling into a fall locks a loss in, and how fast a concentrated position hurts.
    • What it cannot show: real market structure, liquidity, taxes, expense ratios, or how an actual company behaves across a cycle.
    • Not advice. A good score here says nothing about real investing. The point is to feel the mistakes cheaply.

    When you are ready to look at real products, start with the catalogue or the plan guides.
