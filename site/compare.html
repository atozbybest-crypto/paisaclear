How to read this

  • Return type matters more than any headline number: guaranteed, conditional and market-linked products fail in different ways.
  • Lock-in is the real cost of a higher rate. Check what happens if life changes in year two.
  • No rates are shown. Inventing an illustrative rate would make an unverifiable comparison look precise.
  • Nothing here is a recommendation. A product that ranks well on one row can be wrong for your situation.
