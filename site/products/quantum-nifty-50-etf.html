How it works

Quantum Nifty 50 ETF is a etfs & market-linked product from Quantum Mutual Fund, in the market-linked category: no promised return. An exchange-traded fund that holds the fifty largest listed companies in index proportions.

Because the return type is market-linked, the questions that matter most are: What exactly does this hold, and what would make it fall? Can I leave this money invested if it drops 30%?

  • A low-cost way to hold the broad Indian market
  • A building block inside a long-term portfolio

What can go wrong

  • Falls with the market and can stay down for years.
  • Thin trading can push the price away from the underlying value.
  • Brokerage and the bid-ask spread add to the expense ratio.

Before you decide anything

  • Average daily traded volume
