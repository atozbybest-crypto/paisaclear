How it works

LIC's New Jeevan Dhara-II is a lic & life insurance product from Life Insurance Corporation of India, in the guaranteed category: fixed by contract. A deferred annuity with regular or single premiums, where you choose when the pension starts — up to 15 years later.

Because the return type is guaranteed, the questions that matter most are: Is the rate fixed for the whole tenure, or revised during it? Who is actually promising to pay, and what happens if they don't?

  • Choosing a start date for retirement income

Tax
Premiums may qualify for a deduction under Section 80C. Maturity proceeds are exempt only if the policy meets the conditions in Section 10(10D), including the aggregate premium limit — check the current rules for the year you buy.

What can go wrong

  • A long deferment means the money is committed for years.
  • The annuity is fixed in rupees and loses value to inflation.
  • Taking the liquidity option permanently reduces the pension.

Before you decide anything

  • The deferment period you can commit to
  • What a top-up contribution does to the eventual annuity

These are questions to take to the provider, not a checklist that makes a product suitable. Whether something fits your situation is a decision only you — or an adviser you choose — can make.
