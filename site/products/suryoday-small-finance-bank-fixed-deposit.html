How it works

Suryoday Small Finance Bank Fixed Deposit is a bank deposits product from Suryoday Small Finance Bank, in the guaranteed category: fixed by contract. A fixed-term deposit with Suryoday Small Finance Bank. The rate depends on the tenure and the deposit amount, so it is not a single number.

Because the return type is guaranteed, the questions that matter most are: Is the rate fixed for the whole tenure, or revised during it? Who is actually promising to pay, and what happens if they don't?

  • The stable part of a savings plan

What can go wrong

  • The rate is locked for the term, so you lose out if rates rise.
  • Breaking the deposit early reduces the rate you actually earned.
  • Interest is taxed as it accrues, which lowers the effective return.

Before you decide anything

  • The rate for your exact tenure and deposit amount
  • The penalty for premature withdrawal

These are questions to take to the provider, not a checklist that makes a product suitable. Whether something fits your situation is a decision only you — or an adviser you choose — can make.
