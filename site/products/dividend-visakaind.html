What a streak does and does not mean

23 consecutive years of paying a dividend says the board has chosen to return cash every year for a long time. It does not say the next one is coming, or that the amount will hold. A company can cut a dividend in a bad year and keep the historic record intact.

Dividends are quoted per share. What you actually receive depends on how many shares you hold, and the share price moves independently of the payout — a 4% dividend on a stock that fell 20% is not income, it's a smaller loss.
