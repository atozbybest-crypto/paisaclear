How it works

Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana is a government schemes product from Government of India — small savings, in the guaranteed category: fixed by contract. A basic savings account with no minimum balance requirement, opened through any bank or post office.

Because the return type is guaranteed, the questions that matter most are: Is the rate fixed for the whole tenure, or revised during it? Who is actually promising to pay, and what happens if they don't?

  • A first bank account with no balance requirement
  • Receiving government transfers

What can go wrong

  • The rate is typically low, so the balance loses value to inflation.
  • It is a bank account, not an investment.
  • Some accounts attract charges if the balance stays nil for long periods.

Before you decide anything

  • Whether the account comes with a RuPay card and accident cover
  • Any charges for inactivity

These are questions to take to the provider, not a checklist that makes a product suitable. Whether something fits your situation is a decision only you — or an adviser you choose — can make.
