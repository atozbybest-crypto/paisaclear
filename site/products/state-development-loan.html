How it works

State Development Loan (SDL) is a bonds & fixed income product from Government of India — via RBI Retail Direct, in the guaranteed category: fixed by contract. A bond issued by a state government to fund local spending, paying a fixed coupon until maturity.

Because the return type is guaranteed, the questions that matter most are: Is the rate fixed for the whole tenure, or revised during it? Who is actually promising to pay, and what happens if they don't?

  • A slightly higher yield than central government debt
  • Regular semi-annual income

What can go wrong

  • Thinner liquidity than central government bonds, so exit prices can be worse.
  • A fixed coupon for years may lag inflation.
  • State finances differ; do not treat all state issuers as equivalent.

Before you decide anything

  • Which state is issuing, and its borrowing record
  • The difference between the offer price and face value

These are questions to take to the provider, not a checklist that makes a product suitable. Whether something fits your situation is a decision only you — or an adviser you choose — can make.
