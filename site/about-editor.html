About the editor

PaisaClear is written and maintained by one person, Gowtham, based in India. There is no content team, no syndicated feed and no automated text generation on this site.

Last reviewed 15 November 2026

Why this site exists

India has more ways to save than almost anywhere else — small savings schemes, bank deposits, mutual funds, bonds, insurance — and very little plain information about where to start. PaisaClear exists to help you find the products that fit the money you actually have and the features you actually need, with every figure dated and sourced.

A rate printed without a date is not information. It is a guess wearing a suit. PaisaClear exists to publish the figure, the date it was true, and the link to where it came from — for every product on the site, on every page.

How the site is produced

  • Rates are fetched, not typed. Small-savings rates, bond yields and fund NAVs come from official sources on a schedule, and every figure carries its own date.
  • Product records are reviewed by hand. Each record states who it suits, what it costs, what the lock-in is and what to check before buying — written, not scraped.
  • The site is checked daily. An automated audit fails loudly if any published rate has gone stale, if a link breaks, or if a page loses its metadata.
  • Corrections come first. If a figure is wrong, fixing it takes priority over publishing anything new.

What this site will not do

It will not recommend a product, take a payment, sell your data, or publish a number without saying when it was true. The site is not registered with SEBI as an investment adviser, and nothing on it is advice. The disclaimer sets out the limits in full.

How to hold me to it

If you find a figure that is wrong, a rate that has moved, or a claim you think is unsupported, tell me through the contact form. Corrections are published on the page itself, and the date at the top changes when a page is reviewed — so you can see whether what you are reading has been checked recently.

Editorial independence

The site does not currently earn commission from any product it describes. If that changes, the affected pages will say so at the top, and payment will never decide how a product is described or whether it is listed at all.
