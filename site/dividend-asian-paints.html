Asian Paints dividend history: two payments a year, and what they show

Asian Paints pays twice a year — a final dividend after results and an interim later — which is why its payment history looks denser than most Indian companies.

What the record shows

Decorative paints is a high-margin, distribution-heavy business. Asian Paints built its position on a dealer network that competitors have spent a decade trying to replicate, and the payout has historically tracked that profitability rather than growth spending.

The pattern matters more than any single figure: ₹28.15 final in 2024, ₹4.25 interim in November 2024, ₹20.55 final in 2025, ₹4.50 interim, ₹23.00 final in June 2026 and ₹5.75 in November 2026. The final dividend carries most of the payout; the interim is a top-up.

Total payout has been broadly flat to slightly lower over three years, in a period when the company faced new competition in decorative paints. That is the number to watch, not the streak.

The arithmetic

Across these 10 payments the company has paid a total of ₹132.50 per share — an average of ₹13.25 each time, with a low of ₹4.25 and a high of ₹28.15. On a holding of 100 shares, that is about ₹2650 a year at the average rate, before tax.

What it does not tell you

A streak counts years in which something was paid. It says nothing about whether the next payment is safe. Two numbers decide that: the payout ratio against earnings, and free cash flow against the dividend. If the payout exceeds free cash flow for two years running, the record is being funded by the balance sheet.

The two-payment structure means a single year's tally can look volatile when you only look at one payment. Add the final and the interim together before comparing years.

Tax

Dividends are taxed at your slab. The company deducts TDS above ₹5,000 in a year, and you claim the credit when you file. In the 30% slab a ₹1,000 dividend leaves about ₹688 — which is why comparing a dividend yield with a tax-free small-savings rate needs the post-tax number, not the headline.
