The one thing worth knowing first

Indian financial products promise three different kinds of return, and mixing them up is where most beginners get hurt. A guaranteed product fixes the figure in a contract — the risk is that the rate is low and your money is locked. A conditional product pays only if conditions are met. A market-linked product has no promised return at all, and its value moves with a market you do not control.

Every product page here says which of the three applies, when the figure was last checked, and where it came from. If a figure varies by tenure or provider, we link to the provider instead of inventing one.
