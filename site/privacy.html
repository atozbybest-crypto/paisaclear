Privacy

This site has no accounts, no payments and no advertising trackers. It collects very little, and this page says exactly what that is.

Last updated 21 October 2026

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in, no newsletter signup.
  • We set no cookies ourselves. The Google advertising tag may set its own — see below.
  • No advertising or cross-site tracking scripts.
  • What you type into the planners stays in your browser.
  • If you send feedback, we get your message and — only if you type it — your email.

What is collected, and why

Feedback you send us

The feedback form asks for your name, your email, what the message is about, which page it concerns and the message itself. Name, email and the page field are optional. The message is required.

That submission is stored by our hosting provider, Netlify, and sent to the site owner by email. We use it for one purpose: to check the report and, where you asked for a reply, to answer you. We do not use it for marketing and we do not sell it. Submissions are kept until the report is resolved, and for up to twelve months afterwards.

Visit statistics

We use Netlify Analytics, which is built from the hosting provider's own server logs rather than from a script in the page. It records the pages requested, the time, an approximate location derived from the network address, the referring site and the browser type.

It sets no cookies and does not track you across other websites. The figures are aggregated: we see that a page had visitors, not who they were.

The planners and calculators

The salary, savings, loan, emergency-fund and retirement calculators do their arithmetic in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to us, and nothing is stored once you close the page.

The spending planner is the one exception, and it is deliberate: what you log is saved in your browser's local storage on your device so the list survives a reload. It is never transmitted. Clearing your browser data deletes it, and we cannot recover it because we never had it.

Advertising measurement (Google)

We advertise on Google and social platforms, and we use the Google tag (gtag.js, ID AW-18529393816) to measure whether those ads work. When a page loads, the tag sends Google your IP address, the page you are on, the referring site and identifiers stored in cookies, and Google may link that to your Google account if you are signed in.

We see aggregate results — clicks, impressions, cost — not your identity. Google's handling of that data is governed by Google's privacy policy. You can limit ad personalisation in Google My Ad Center, or block the tag with any content blocker.

Search and hosting

The site is served by Netlify, which necessarily processes the request when you load a page. Search Console is used to see which queries bring people here from Google — that data is aggregate search performance, not a record of individual visitors.

Cookies

We set no cookies of our own. The Google tag described above may set cookies used to measure advertising; those are Google's, not ours, and are covered by their policy. If we add anything else, this page changes first.

Your choices

  • Ask what we hold. Send a feedback message and we will tell you what was received from you.
  • Ask us to delete it. We will remove any feedback submission and anything derived from it.
  • Correct it. Tell us which field is wrong and we will fix or delete the record.
  • Withdraw. Any reply you asked for stops when you ask us to stop.

Use the feedback form for any of these, and mention that your message is a privacy request so it is handled separately from corrections.

What this site does not do

  • No selling or renting of personal data.
  • No advertising profiles built by us. The Google tag measures ad performance; we never see who you are.
  • No tracking pixels embedded by third parties.
  • No payments, so no card or bank details are ever collected.
  • No accounts, so there are no passwords for us to hold.

Children

The site explains financial products for adults considering them. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this page

If what the site collects changes — for example, if a newsletter is ever added — this page is updated before the change goes live, and the date at the top changes with it.

Questions about any of this can go through the feedback form. That is the only contact route, and it is deliberately the only one.
