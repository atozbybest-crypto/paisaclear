Scam checker

Paste a message, a link or a number you are unsure about. We highlight everything that appears in known financial scams — then you verify through the official channel. This is a red-flag checker, not a verdict.

Runs in your browser · nothing is sent anywhere

Paste the message as it arrived. The more of it you include, the more patterns we can match.

Nothing checked yet.

What this can and cannot do

  • It recognises the wording, links and payment requests that appear in real scams — twenty-four patterns, weighted by how dangerous each one is.
  • It cannot tell you something is genuine. A low score means the usual patterns are absent, not that the message is safe.
  • It cannot check a phone number against a call database. There is no public list of scam numbers in India — only your bank and the cybercrime portal can act on one.
  • Always verify by calling the number printed on your card or the one on the company's own website — never a number inside the message.
