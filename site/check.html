Scam checker

Paste a message, a phone number or a link you are unsure about. We highlight the patterns that appear in known scams — then you verify through the official channel. This is a red-flag checker, not a verdict.

Nothing checked yet.

What this can and cannot do

  • It recognises wording and link patterns that appear in real scams: urgency, OTP requests, lookalike domains, UPI collect requests.
  • It cannot tell you something is genuine. A clean result only means the usual patterns are absent — not that the message is safe.
  • It has no access to call databases, bank records or the RBI fraud registry. Those are the only sources that can confirm a number or account.
  • Always verify by calling the number printed on your bank card or on the bank's own website — never a number in the message.

Reporting: cybercrime.gov.in or 1930 · tell us about a scam you received
