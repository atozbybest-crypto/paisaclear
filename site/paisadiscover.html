How this works, and what it is not

  • It filters on facts we hold: minimum investment, lock-in, payout frequency, risk type and tax treatment.
  • Every result states the reason it appeared, so you can disagree with the logic rather than trust a black box.
  • It does not know your income, debts, dependants or goals. It cannot tell you what to buy, and it does not try.
  • A product appearing here is not a suitability judgement. Read the tax, lock-in and risk sections on its page.
