Income tax calculator

Enter your net taxable income and see the tax, rebate, surcharge and cess. Individual, HUF, firm, company and local authority, for the 2026-27 tax year.

As per the Income-tax Act, 2025 (amended by Finance Act, 2026)

What this covers, and what it doesn't

  • Individual and HUF under both regimes, with the higher exemption for senior citizens under the old regime.
  • Firm/LLP, domestic company and local authority at their flat rates, with surcharge and 4% cess.
  • It takes net taxable income, not gross salary — deduct exemptions and 80C-80U first. The salary calculator does that part for salaried people.
  • Marginal relief on surcharge is not applied, and co-operative societies, trusts and special-rate incomes (capital gains, 115BBH and similar) are not handled.

Rule set dated 2026-10-19. Verify at the Income Tax Department calculator.
