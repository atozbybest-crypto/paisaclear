How to read these numbers

  • Every input is yours. Change the months of cover, the returns, the inflation — the result follows immediately.
  • The product list is filtered by minimum investment only: your amount either reaches it or it doesn't.
  • No return is promised. A 10% assumption is an assumption, and a bad year is not averaged away.
  • Tax is ignored. So are charges, exit loads and the years when a fund falls.
