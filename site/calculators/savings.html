Your numbers

Read this before trusting the number

  • The arithmetic is exact: compounded at the rate you typed, for the term you typed.
  • No fund earns the same rate every year. A 12% average can include a 30% fall.
  • Tax, exit loads and expense ratios are not deducted. A 6% deposit and a 12% fund are not comparable until they are.
  • Deposit rates change. Check the bank's rate card for the tenure you actually want.
