Standard deduction ₹75,000 · Section 87A rebate up to ₹60,000, which makes taxable income up to ₹12,00,000 tax-free · 4% cess · surcharge above ₹50 lakh.

What this does and doesn't do

  • Compares the two regimes on the same salary, so the difference is like for like.
  • Uses the slabs in force for FY 2026-27, including the standard deduction, rebate, surcharge and 4% cess.
  • It is an estimate, not a tax computation. It ignores marginal relief on surcharge, gratuity, LTA, employer NPS, leave encashment and any income outside salary.
  • HRA uses the standard three-part test. Special cases — your own house, shared rent, non-metro city definitions — need a tax adviser.
  • Slabs change with each Budget. The date above is the version encoded here.
