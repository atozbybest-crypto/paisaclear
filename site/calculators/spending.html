      What this is, and isn't

      • Your entries are stored in this browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere, and there is no account.
      • Clearing your browser data deletes the list — there is no copy on a server.
      • The product list shows what your balance can reach by minimum investment. It is not a recommendation, and it ignores your goals, tax position and risk capacity.
      • Categories are a simple aid to seeing patterns, not a budget you have to keep.
