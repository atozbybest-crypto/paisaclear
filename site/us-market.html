Before you invest from India

  • Remitting money abroad falls under the RBI's liberalised remittance scheme, with a TCS threshold you should check for the current year.
  • US dividends are taxed at source, and India gives a foreign tax credit — the paperwork matters.
  • Estate tax. US-situated shares held by a non-resident can be exposed to US estate tax above a low threshold. This catches Indian investors by surprise.
  • Currency moves can wipe out a gain that looks good in dollars.

What this page is not

  • Not a recommendation, and not a comparison of expense ratios, tracking error or liquidity.
  • Not tax advice. Cross-border treatment depends on your residency and on rules that change.
  • Every figure carries its date. Prices move continuously; a page is a snapshot.
