Before investing from India

  • Remittances fall under the RBI's liberalised remittance scheme, with a TCS threshold that changes — check the current rule.
  • US dividends are withheld at source; India gives a foreign tax credit, and the paperwork decides whether you actually get it.
  • Estate tax. US-situated shares held by a non-resident can be exposed above a low threshold. The cross-border guide explains it.
  • Returns here are in dollars. Your return in rupees includes the exchange rate.
