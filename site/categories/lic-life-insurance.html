LIC & Life Insurance Policies

Life insurance policies from LIC and its GIFT City branch. A policy mixes two things that are priced separately elsewhere: a death benefit, and a savings or pension component. The premium depends on your age, the term and the sum assured, so it is never a single number.

What to check first

  • What the policy pays if you die, and when
  • What you get back if you survive the term — and what you get if you stop paying
  • The surrender value after each year, and how long the money is actually locked
  • Whether a term plan plus a separate investment would do the same job for less
